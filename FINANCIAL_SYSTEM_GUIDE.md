### Conservative Income Calculation

The household uses a **conservative income model** for budgeting that assumes only 50% of stock value:

```
Conservative Income = Net Cash + (Net Stock × 50%)
```

**2026 Conservative Income:**
- Total Net Cash: $272,688.43
- Total Net Stock (at 49% tax): $419,131.01
- 50% Stock Value: $190,235.17
- **Total Conservative Income: $462,923.60/year ($38,576.97/month)**

**Why 50% Stock Value?**
- Stock price volatility means actual value may be lower at vest time
- Provides cushion against market downturns
- Any stock value above 50% becomes "bonus money"

### Tax Assumptions

- **Federal + State Tax Rate:** 49% (increased from 47% in 2025)
- Calculated using SmartAsset tax calculator
- Actual effective rate may vary based on deductions and timing

## Bonus Money Allocation

Income above the conservative baseline is considered "bonus money" and allocated using a specific split:

### 2026 Allocation (Updated)

| Category | Percentage | Purpose |
|----------|------------|---------|
| Investments | 70% | Vanguard brokerage, additional 529 |
| Savings | 18% | High-yield savings, planned purchases |
| Fun Money | 5% | Split equally between Anand & Anchita |
| Travel | 7% | Dedicated travel fund from bonus |

### Bonus Income Sources

1. **RSU Vests (Feb/May/Aug/Nov):** The portion above 50% stock value
2. **Cash Bonus (March):** ~$57K annual bonus
3. **Stock Price Appreciation:** If META trades above projection price

### Travel Budget Calculation

**Important:** Travel is NOT a fixed monthly budget. It's funded from 7% of bonus income as it arrives.

- **Until first bonus vests:** Travel budget = $0
- **Travel spending before bonus:** Shows as negative balance
- **After bonuses vest:** Travel budget accumulates from Bonus Log

### Example Calculation

If February RSU vest yields $165,898 net (but only $75,059 was in conservative budget):
- Bonus Amount: $165,898 - $75,059 = $90,839
- To Investments (70%): $63,587
- To Savings (18%): $16,351
- To Fun Money (5%): $4,542 ($2,271 each)
- To Travel (7%): $6,359

## Monthly Review Process

### On-Request Update Process

When the user requests a monthly review:

1. **Export Transactions:**
   - User exports fresh `Transactions.csv` from Copilot Money
   - Place in project root directory

2. **Claude Analysis:**
   - Read Transactions.csv
   - Filter for current month
   - Categorize using mapping above
   - Compare to budget targets

3. **Update Google Sheet:**
   - Update Monthly Tracker tab with actual spending
   - Update Fun Money balances
   - Log any bonus allocations

4. **Generate Report:**
   - Budget vs actual variance
   - Category breakdown
   - Notable transactions
   - Recommendations

### Quarterly Deep Review

Every quarter (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec):

1. Full YTD analysis
2. Investment progress check
3. Fun Money reconciliation
4. Budget adjustment recommendations
5. Travel spending review

### Key Questions the System Answers

1. **"How are we doing on our budgets right now?"**
   - Pull transactions, categorize by budget type
   - Compare against monthly targets

2. **"How much Fun Money do I have left? What about my wife?"**
   - Check Cashflow tab for current balances
   - Subtract recent Fun Money transactions

3. **"How much is our current savings?"**
   - Sum savings account balances
   - Reference savings goals

4. **"How much can we afford to spend on vacations this year?"**
   - Calculate Travel budget from bonus allocation
   - Subtract already-spent travel costs
